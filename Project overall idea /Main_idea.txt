TRUSTED ISSUER SIDE

Issuer creates original PDF
        ↓
Uploads PDF to QRTrust
        ↓
QRTrust calculates SHA-256 hash
        ↓
QRTrust signs the hash with issuer's private key
        ↓
Creates unique Document ID
        ↓
Stores:
- document hash
- signature
- issuer info
- status
        ↓
Generates QR code
        ↓
QR code is placed on document
